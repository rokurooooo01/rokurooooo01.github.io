Foundational Mathematics

by rokurooooo · two volumes

Free open math textbooks.

Foundational Mathematics and Foundational Mathematics for Social Sciences are my publication projects that I've been doing for long. Derived from my scrapped The Explanation project, these books uses PASUM syllabus as the backbone, with explanations to the best that I can. Plus, I don't know what to do during this break so I figured out why not making these. Now available for free download. More to come.

Free to read, share, remix under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Contents

  1. Chapter 1 - Number System. Rational and irrational numbers, set and interval notation, indices, surds, and logarithms
  2. Chapter 2 - Functions. Domains and ranges, common families, transformations, composite and piecewise functions, and inverses
  3. Chapter 3 - Polynomials. Arithmetic, division, factorisation, the remainder and factor theorems, and partial fractions (needed again in integral calculus).
  4. Chapter 4 - Inequalities. Quadratic, cubic, rational, and absolute-value inequalities.
  5. Chapter 5 - Matrices. Operations, determinants, inverses, and systems of simultaneous linear equations. The lying foundations of linear algebra for graphics and machine learning.
  6. Chapter 6 - Trigonometry. Angle measure, the basic functions, graphs, identities, equations, and inverses.
  7. Chapter 7 - Linearisation and Linear Law. Turning non-linear relationships into straight-line form: scatter plots, best fit, gradients and intercepts in models.
  8. Chapter 8 - Coordinate Geometry and Conic Sections. Parabolas, ellipses, hyperbolas, and parametric equations.
  9. Chapter 9 - Probability and Statistics. Permutations and combinations; the binomial, normal, Poisson, and exponential distributions.
  10. Chapter 10 - Complex Numbers. Algebraic, polar, exponential, and geometric forms, with De Moivre's theorem and Euler's formula.
  11. Chapter 11 - Hyperbolic Functions. Identities, graphs, and inverses, alongside comparisons with the circular functions of Chapter 6.
  12. Chapter 12 - Differential Calculus. Limits and first principles, the standard rules, and applications to rates of change and optimisation.
  13. Chapter 13 - Integral Calculus. Integration as the inverse of differentiation: methods, definite integrals, accumulated quantities and areas.
  14. Chapter 14 - Differential Equations. First-order equations and models of growth, decay, mixing, and continuous change.
  15. Chapter 15 - Sequences and Series. Convergence, binomial and power series, Taylor and Maclaurin expansions.
  16. Chapter 16 - Vectors. Two and three dimensions: operations, products, relative motion, vector-valued differentiation and integration.
  17. Chapter 17 - Introduction to Graph Theory. Vertices and edges, paths, cycles, trees, planar graphs, colouring, shortest paths, spanning trees — discrete maths for CS and networks.
  18. Chapter 18 - Introduction to Logical Reasoning. Propositions, truth tables, implications, quantifiers, valid arguments, fallacies, and elementary proof methods.
  19. Chapter 19 (bonus) - Computing with R. Vectors, data frames, importing and visualising data, probability distributions, one-sample tests, and matrix calculations — applying Chapters 5 and 9 computationally.
