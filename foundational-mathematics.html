Foundational Mathematics

by rokurooooo · first release

A small textbook that grows from counting to change.

Foundational Mathematics starts with arithmetic, builds the number systems, crosses the bridge of limits and continuity, and arrives at differential equations. Each chapter is a page on this site.

This is v0.1.0 — releasing 20 Sept 2026. Free to read, share, remix under CC BY-SA 4.0.
